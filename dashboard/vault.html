Deposit

Put capital under the agent

Deposit mock USDC and the agent manages it alongside everything else in the vault. You receive shares whose value moves as premium accrues and as catastrophes erode collateral, so your balance can fall. That is what backing reinsurance means.

Step 1

Connect a wallet

Step 2

Get mock USDC

An open faucet mints 10,000 to whoever asks. A test token, no value. Gas comes from the X Layer faucet.

Deposit

Approve the vault, then deposit. Two transactions the first time, one after that.
